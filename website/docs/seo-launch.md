# Search and publishing guide

BCA uses page-specific titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter previews, Organization/WebSite/project/article structured data, a sitemap with concept images and crawler directives.

## Domain and discovery

Maintain the canonical public origin through `siteOrigin` in `lib/seo.ts`. When changing the domain, update canonical settings and configure redirects from the previous public origin where supported. Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit `/sitemap.xml` and inspect representative pages.

Search engines need public access to crawl brochure pages. Administrator pages, private project rooms and draft policies are excluded from indexing. Application authorization continues to protect private data independently of crawler rules.

## Content direction

Publish original material about BCA’s design process, automotive concepts, mobility research and collaboration opportunities. Relevant themes include automotive design studios in Nigeria, African automotive design, commissioned vehicle concepts, automotive design IP, CNG/electric sedan concepts, passenger-cargo transport and SUV/pickup concept design.

Keep the studio’s development claims aligned with evidence. Avoid keyword stuffing, fabricated reviews, paid link schemes or unsupported manufacturing claims. Link the website from approved company profiles and build legitimate industry references.

## Review

Monitor indexed URLs, search impressions and qualified enquiries. Check mobile rendering, Core Web Vitals, canonicals and structured data when releasing changes. Search optimization supports discovery and relevance; it does not guarantee ranking positions.
